## 6. Visual Direction

Use the supplied GLOAM cinematic design brief as a reference: - Dark
slate, bone white, frost blue, restrained forest green. - Geist or
similar sans-serif. - Cinematic dark hero, readable scrim, premium glass
navigation where suitable. - Subtle animation and reduced-motion
support. - Responsive mobile menu. - Clear contrast and accessible
interactions.

The corporate website may use lighter layouts for long service details.
The GLOAM source's prohibition on React/Tailwind does not apply to this
project; the requested project stack is React, TypeScript, and Tailwind.

## 8. Content Integrity Rules

Never invent: - Client names or logos - Testimonials or reviews - Number
of customers, staff, projects, or years of experience beyond the
confirmed establishment date - Awards, certificates, accreditations, or
regulatory approvals - Exact service guarantees, response times,
pricing, or coverage areas - Specific products/services under the
additional activity categories unless confirmed

If information is missing, write a neutral description or add a TODO for
client confirmation.

## 9. Contact Form Truthfulness

A client-side form is not a functioning enquiry channel by itself. Do
not display a false "sent successfully" message unless the request was
actually accepted by a configured service. If there is no backend, use a
clearly identified integration point or an intentional mailto fallback.
